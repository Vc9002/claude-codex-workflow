# Legal Studies and History Minor (Penn)

Source: Wharton Legal Studies & Business Ethics page (2026). Vincent is declared or planning this minor. Students declare through their home school.

## Total
- 8 CU total: 3 required courses + 5 elective-bracket courses.
- 4 courses from Wharton, 4 from the College of Arts & Sciences.

## Required (all three)
- **LGST 1010** Law and Social Values (previously LGST 101)
- **HIST 1119** History of American Law to 1877 (previously HIST 168)
- **HIST 1169** History of American Law since 1877 (previously HIST 169)

## Elective brackets

Pre-2024 structure (still listed on the page, marked as the requirement for earlier cohorts):

1. **Philosophical Foundations** (choose 1): ECON 0460, ECON 4440, HIST 1201, HIST 1225, HIST 2202, HIST 3202, PHIL/PPE 1443, PHIL 1450, PHIL 2450, PSCI 0602 (only one requirement)
2. **Political and Institutional Context** (choose 1): AFRC 3500, HIST 1110, HIST 1125, HIST 1153, HIST 1166, HIST 1172, HIST 1180, HIST 1733, HIST 1740, HIST 2159, HIST 3910, PSCI 0602 (only one requirement), SOCI 1120
3. **International Perspective** (choose 1): LGST 2160, LGST 2190, LGST 2240
4. **Public Policy & Regulation** (choose 1): BEPP 2010, BEPP 2030, LGST 1000
5. **Legal Depth** (choose 1): LGST 2020, 2040, 2050, 2080, 2130, 2150, 2180, 2220, 2280, 2430, 2440

Current structure (students declaring LSHS in PATH in Fall 2024 and later, and the Class of 2025 and later, per the page's note):
- **Legal Depth: choose 3 courses** from the combined list: BEPP 2010, BEPP 2030, LGST 1000, LGST 2020, 2040, 2050, 2080, 2130, 2140, 2150, 2160, 2180, 2190, 2220, 2240, 2280, 2430, 2440.

Confirm which structure applies to Vincent's cohort with the LSHS advisor before planning.
