# Penn Political Science, BA: Political Economy

Source: Penn Undergraduate Catalog 2026-27 (entering Fall 2026 and later). Students should confirm final certification with their academic program.

## Total
- 32 course units for graduation in this major (double majors may need more).
- Minimum 9 CU in Political Science, of which 6 must be taken in the Penn Political Science Department (includes the Washington, DC semester).
- Transfer and AP credit restrictions apply (see Major Program Booklet).

## Requirement breakdown

| Block | CU | What counts |
|---|---|---|
| College General Education + Free Electives | 20 | Foundational Approaches + Sectors + free electives (not part of the major) |
| Core: comparative politics | 1 | PSCI 0100 Introduction to Comparative Politics, or PSCI 0101 Comparative Politics of Developing Areas |
| Core: second course | 1 | One of PSCI 0200 (Intro to American Politics), PSCI 0400 (Intro to International Relations), PSCI 0600 (Ancient Political Thought), or PSCI 0601 (Modern Political Thought) |
| Subfield: IPE | 1 | PSCI 1402 International Political Economy (required) |
| Subfield: two courses | 2 | Two courses in two of three subfields: American Politics (attribute APSA), Comparative Politics (APSC), Political Theory (APST) |
| PSCI or Major-Related Electives | 7 | Any PSCI or major-related electives; max 3 non-PSCI courses |

Major total: 32 CU, counting the core, subfield, and elective blocks.

## Political Economy concentration
- 5 CU from the major must carry attribute **APSE** (Political Economy), chosen with the major advisor.
- Those 5 must include PSCI 0100 or 0101 (as one of the core courses) and PSCI 1402 (as the IR subfield course).
- At least 3 of the 5 concentration courses must be PSCI courses.

## Honors
- Requires overall GPA of at least 3.3 and at least 3.6 GPA in PSCI courses taken in the major.
- Honors courses: PSCI 4997 Political Science Honors (1 CU), PSCI 4999 Honors Thesis Independent Study (1 CU).

## Gen-ed attribute codes relevant to PSCI
- APSA: PSCI American Politics
- APSC per the catalog (Comparative Politics). The Penn Course tool's gen-ed reference lists this as APCP, so verify which code the course search actually uses.
- APSI: PSCI International Relations
- APST: PSCI Political Theory
- APSE: Political Economy concentration. Not in the tool's gen-ed reference list, so verify in course search.

## Notes
- A course can count toward the major and a Sector requirement only once (see Policy Statement for exceptions).
- The Penn Course Review and Path tools can confirm attributes and availability for a given semester (2027A = Spring 2027, 2026C = Fall 2026).
